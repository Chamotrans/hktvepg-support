HK TV EPG 私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 7 月 18 日

我哋唔收集任何資料

HK TV EPG 唔會收集、儲存或分享任何個人資料。App 冇用戶帳號、冇分析追蹤、冇廣告 SDK、冇第三方數據收集。

網絡使用

App 只會喺更新節目表時連接公開 EPG 資料來源（epg.pw 及 myeth-epg.github.io）下載節目資料。呢啲請求唔包含任何可識別你身份嘅資料。

本機儲存

節目表快取同你嘅最愛頻道只儲存喺你部裝置入面，唔會上傳到任何伺服器。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 18 July 2026

We collect no data. HK TV EPG does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party data collection.

Network usage. The app connects to public EPG data sources (epg.pw and myeth-epg.github.io) only to download TV programme listings. These requests contain no personally identifiable information.

Local storage. The programme cache and your favourite channels are stored only on your device and never uploaded to any server.
